3. Devices, setup, and connections

You need a compatible iPhone, a supported Mac with Remote Login enabled, and a network route between them. MARC currently supports iOS 18 or later. Access beyond your local network requires separate secure networking; MARC does not provide a hosted relay.

Some controls need helper tools, macOS permissions or compatible hardware. External displays and audio devices may not expose adjustable brightness or volume. I cannot promise that every control works on every device or macOS version.

Keep your login details and devices secure. Check SSH host-key fingerprints when prompted, and investigate an unexpected key change before trusting it.

4. Commands, files, and process actions

Terminal commands execute on your Mac using your connected account. File uploads, replacements, moves, permission changes and deletions can change or remove real data. Terminating a process can stop work or close an app.

Check the target and confirmation before taking an action. Keep backups of important files. A canceled request or interrupted connection cannot always undo an operation that the Mac has already completed.

AI is optional. Local mode uses Ollama on your selected Mac; API mode uses the endpoint and key you configure. Provider access, availability, quotas, charges and data handling are controlled by that provider and your account.

AI can suggest incorrect, unsuitable or destructive commands. Read each suggestion before tapping Run. MARC does not automatically execute a generated command and cannot guarantee its safety or correctness.

Do not send information you are not authorized to share. Third-party services have their own terms. The Privacy Policy explains what MARC sends and how to stop future AI requests.
