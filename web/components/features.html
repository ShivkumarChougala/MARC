Why I built MARC

Your Mac.
Your access.

Your files, your terminal, your machine. Accessing them shouldn’t mean another subscription.

I built MARC to bring everyday Mac tools to your iPhone without putting basic access behind a paywall. From adjusting the volume to opening multiple terminal sessions, your Mac stays within reach.

Your files, fully accessible.

Browse, preview, upload, download, rename, move, and change permissions from your iPhone.

More room for your terminal.

Run commands, keep multiple SSH sessions open, and get optional AI suggestions you review before running.

Small controls. Useful freedom.

Adjust volume and brightness, control media, launch apps, and check what’s running.

Your connection. Your choice.

Connect directly over SSH. No MARC account, no ads, and no subscription needed for core access.

Optional AI providers may charge for API usage.
