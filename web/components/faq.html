Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Mac monitoring, file management, multiple terminal sessions, and everyday controls don’t require a subscription.

How does MARC connect to my Mac?

MARC connects over SSH. Enable Remote Login on your Mac, then add it in the app using your Mac credentials or an SSH key.

Can I use MARC away from home?

Yes, when your iPhone can reach your Mac over the network. For access outside your home network, you can use a VPN that connects your devices.

Do I need to create an account?

No MARC account is required. Connect directly using your Mac’s login details or an SSH key.

Is AI required?

No. AI assistance is optional. You can connect a local AI model running on your Mac or use a supported API provider. You review suggested commands before running them. API providers may charge for usage.

Can I control my Mac’s screen?

MARC provides monitoring, file management, terminal access, and supported controls. It doesn’t stream your Mac’s desktop.
