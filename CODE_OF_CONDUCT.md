# Code of Conduct

MARC welcomes people who want to use, understand, and improve the project. You do not need to be an experienced developer to participate.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Give feedback about the work, with enough context to make it useful.
- Be patient with questions and people learning something new.
- Respect personal boundaries and requests to stop a conversation.
- Protect other people’s privacy. Ask before sharing their messages or personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Neither are spam or repeated attempts to disrupt a discussion.

## Where this applies

This code applies to MARC’s issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainer at **contact@getmarc.app**. Include a link or description of what happened and any relevant context. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Details may need to be shared with people involved in reviewing or addressing the concern.

## How concerns are handled

The maintainer will review the available context and decide what action is appropriate. Depending on the situation, this may include asking someone to change their behavior, removing content, restricting participation, or blocking an account.

The goal is to keep participation safe and constructive. Serious incidents may result in immediate restrictions. A person affected by a moderation decision can contact the maintainer to request a review.

This code applies to everyone participating in the project, including maintainers.
